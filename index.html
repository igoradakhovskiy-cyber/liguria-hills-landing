The Mediterranean way of living

Finale Ligure is one of Europe’s leading outdoor destinations, where climate, coastline and sport meet in one of Italy’s most beautiful coastal towns.

Sunny days a year
265
Climbing routes
3,000+
MTB and hiking trails
200+
Minutes to the sea

From sea-level beaches to mountain-top trails, life here runs all year and mixes rest with adventure, culture with nature.

  • Rock climbing

    Over 3,000 routes across limestone cliffs: Finale is a global climbing capital.

  • Mountain biking

    More than 200 trails through Mediterranean forest and along coastal ridges, with renowned enduro terrain.

  • Hiking and trekking

    Ancient paths linking hilltop villages, panoramic ridges and hidden coves.

  • Sea and beaches

    Clear water, sandy beaches and a lively promenade 15 minutes away.

  • Ligurian cuisine

    Fresh pesto, focaccia, seafood and local wines in seaside trattorias.

  • Culture and heritage

    Medieval villages, Baroque churches and a rich artistic heritage along the Riviera.

Every season has its charm

Spring

Mild temperatures, blooming hillsides and the start of the outdoor season. Perfect for hiking and climbing.

Summer

Long sunny days on the beach, the evening passeggiata along the coast and lively local festivals.

Autumn

Golden light, harvest time, quiet trails and the best climbing conditions of the year.

Winter

Mild Mediterranean winters with clear skies: mountain biking, coastal walks and everyday Italian life.
